Aerodynamics notes (edited live)

Lift is generated when air flows faster over the top of a wing than underneath it.

Bernoulli's principle

Faster-moving air has lower pressure. This creates a pressure difference between the wing's upper and lower surfaces.

Newton's third law

The wing also deflects air downward (downwash), and the reaction force pushes the wing up.

  • Angle of attack increases lift, up to the stall angle
  • Past the stall angle, airflow separates and lift collapses
